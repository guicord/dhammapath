# Story 01: Explore the concept map

## User story
As a learner, I want to view a polished Dhamma concept map so that I can understand the major teachings and how they connect.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app shows a main concept map view on first entry.
- The map visually groups major teaching areas such as suffering, path, ethics, concentration, and liberation.
- Users can identify key concepts and relationships without reading long text blocks.
- The map remains readable on desktop and mobile layouts.
- The layout preserves the intended curated visual style and theme system.

## Notes
- This is the primary entry point for the product.
- The experience should feel calm, structured, and easy to explore.
- This story directly supports the visual identity of the app.
