# Story 04: Read a clear explanation

## User story
As a learner, I want to read a clear explanation of each concept so that I can understand its meaning and significance.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each concept page includes a short summary and fuller explanation.
- Explanations are written in plain language and are easy to scan.
- Concepts are presented consistently across the app.
- The explanation does not rely on technical jargon that prevents understanding.

## Notes
- Explanations should be concise but substantive.
- This is the main educational content layer.
