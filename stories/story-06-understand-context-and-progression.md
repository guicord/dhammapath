# Story 06: Understand context and progression

## User story
As a learner, I want to see the learning progression around a concept so that I can understand what comes before and after it.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each concept page shows prerequisite or related concepts when available.
- The product indicates when a concept is foundational, intermediate, or advanced.
- Users can move through a concept sequence without getting lost.
- Concepts are organized in a way that supports gradual understanding.

## Notes
- Progression supports learning flow and reduces cognitive overload.
- The concept graph should help users see the learning order.
