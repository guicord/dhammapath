# Story 07: Track learned concepts

## User story
As a learner, I want to mark concepts as learned so that I can track my progress over time.

## Acceptance criteria
- Users can mark a concept as learned or not learned.
- Learning status is stored against the user.
- The status is visible on concept pages and in progress views.
- Users can revisit a concept and change its status if needed.

## Notes
- Progress tracking is essential for retention and motivation.
- Status should be simple and low-friction to update.
