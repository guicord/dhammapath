# Story 08: Check understanding with quizzes

## User story
As a learner, I want to answer short quizzes so that I can check whether I understand the teaching.

## Acceptance criteria
- A concept page includes a quiz or review section when applicable.
- Quiz questions are short and directly relevant to the concept.
- Users receive immediate feedback after answering.
- Incorrect answers include explanatory guidance or a prompt to revisit the concept.
- Quiz results are tracked against the user.

## Notes
- Quizzes create reinforcement and active learning.
- The goal is comprehension, not simply completion.
