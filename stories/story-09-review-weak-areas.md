# Story 09: Review weak areas

## User story
As a learner, I want to revisit concepts I have not mastered so that I can improve my understanding over time.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app can identify concepts that have not been mastered.
- Users can access a review or recap list.
- Review content prioritizes concepts with low mastery or incomplete progress.
- The system supports repetition without requiring manual management by the user.

## Notes
- This supports retention and spaced review.
- It keeps the app useful beyond a single pass through the content.
