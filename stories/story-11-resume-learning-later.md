# Story 11: Resume learning later

## User story
As a returning learner, I want to log in and continue my study so that I can pick up where I left off.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app restores the user’s progress after login.
- Learned concepts and quiz history remain associated with the account.
- The app presents a clear path for the user to continue learning.
- The app keeps the experience stable across sessions.

## Notes
- This directly supports habit formation and ongoing study.
- Persistence matters even for a small learning app.
