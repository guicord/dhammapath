# Story 13: Maintain the curated design while scaling content

## User story
As a product owner, I want the curated visual map to stay polished even as the concept library grows so that the app remains useful and attractive.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app keeps the handcrafted visual map as the main overview experience.
- The database powers content growth without removing the design quality.
- Concept categories and visual themes remain consistent as new content is added.
- New concepts can be assigned to existing groups or new groups without breaking the current layout.

## Notes
- This story protects the product’s visual identity.
- It ensures the design and the data model evolve together.
