# Story 14: Study content in context

## User story
As a learner, I want each concept connected to its relevant source references so that I can study the Dhamma with confidence and context.

## Acceptance criteria
- Source material is connected to the appropriate concept records.
- The user can view source material without leaving the learning flow.
- The app clearly distinguishes between teaching summaries and original texts.
- Source links are maintained and easy to update.

## Notes
- Source linking supports trust and credibility.
- This is important for a teaching app, especially one grounded in canonical texts.
