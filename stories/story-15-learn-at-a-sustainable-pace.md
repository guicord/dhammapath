# Story 15: Learn at a sustainable pace

## User story
As a learner, I want a structured learning experience so that I can build understanding gradually instead of feeling overwhelmed.

## Acceptance criteria
- The app supports a learning journey with clear progression.
- Users can explore concepts by theme, difficulty, or relationship.
- The interface does not overwhelm the learner with too much information at once.
- The experience supports both beginner exploration and deeper study.

## Notes
- The experience should feel supportive, not dense or intimidating.
- Sustainable pacing is a key product quality for Dhamma study.
