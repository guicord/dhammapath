# Story 16: Understand what this map is before diving in

## User story
As a product owner, I want first-time visitors to understand what the map represents and how to read it before they reach the content, so that newcomers aren't dropped into dense terminology with no framing.

## Acceptance criteria
- The map states, in plain language and without requiring interaction, what tradition and scope it represents before the first content section.
- The meaning of "Dhamma" is visible without depending on a visitor discovering that the masthead title is itself interactive.
- The existing interaction hint is extended into a short legend covering all the map's affordances (underlined term, arrow, flip card), not just the hover/tap mechanic.
- The addition does not require a dismissible modal or block access to the map on first load.

## Notes
- Supports story 1's requirement that the experience "feel calm, structured, and easy to explore" — orientation should sit on the page, not interrupt it.
- Keep this to a few sentences; the goal is orientation, not a lesson in itself.
