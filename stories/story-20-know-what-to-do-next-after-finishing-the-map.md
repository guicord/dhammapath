# Story 20: Know what to do next after finishing the map

## User story
As a product owner, I want the end of the map to point the learner somewhere, so that finishing the content doesn't feel like a dead end.

## Acceptance criteria
- After the final section of page 2, the learner is offered at least one clear next step (e.g. return to page 1, browse the concept index, or an honest note that progress tracking and quizzes are planned).
- The closing moment does not silently end with no call to action.

## Notes
- A placeholder next step is acceptable until stories 7–11 (progress tracking, quizzes, accounts) are built; the goal now is to avoid an abrupt stop, not to pre-build those features.
